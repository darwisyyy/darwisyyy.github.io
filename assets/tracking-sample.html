What the finding means

In a real run, detecting an outgoing request establishes that the browser sent that request to the observed destination. It does not establish vendor acceptance, attribution, server-side delivery or a successful business outcome.

If capture is incomplete or a control is unavailable, the report preserves that limit instead of inventing a pass or declaring a missing event conclusively.

This standalone illustration demonstrates the evidence model; it is not a replacement for the tool’s full audit report.
